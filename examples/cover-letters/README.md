# Reviewed cover letter examples

These three examples illustrate successful choices in evidence, attribution, structure and voice. They are contribution lenses, not ideal letters, fixed templates, or an exhaustive taxonomy.

- [Research Scientist](research-scientist.md): a modeling question, experiment authorship, personal contribution and measured result.
- [Research Engineer or Applied Scientist](research-engineer.md): physical modeling, owned implementation, integration and useful engineering decisions.
- [Technical Lead](technical-lead.md): agenda, reusable scientific software, contributor review and measurement judgment.

## How to use them

Choose the current reader's main uncertainty and the evidence first. Consult these examples for editorial comparison after an initial argument or draft exists. Their facts are not an independent evidence source: verify selected claims and current boundaries in the resume bullet bank. Do not turn a good phrase, employer connection or personal motivation into universal wording.

Both a skeptical technical hiring consultant and a separate voice editor reviewed these drafts, and their corrections were incorporated. The drafts mainly refine already strong letters; that review does not prove each is better than its predecessor or suitable for every role. The relevant standard is whether the selected evidence answers the new reader's question in Kevin's voice.

The Markdown letters and notes are intended for version control. Generated Word files, selection plans and rendering intermediates are local review artifacts under `.local/cover-letter-system-review/`, not tracked examples. Historical application letters remain untouched. No private phone, location or email contact block is included here.
