## Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Machine Learning Researcher position at D. E. Shaw. The role's emphasis on developing modeling ideas from an initial hypothesis through implementation and testing is the work I want to pursue. My background combines statistical modeling and deep time-series learning, with particular attention to whether a result survives a credible evaluation.

At Cohen Veterans Bioscience, I conceived ablation experiments comparing an end-to-end Conv1D-LSTM approach with a classical signal-processing, handcrafted-feature, and SVM pipeline. I delegated most implementation to a direct report and personally contributed the final, best-performing architecture. In separate gesture-recognition work, I found overlapping-window leakage and time-ordering bias, then redesigned validation around leave-one-subject-out testing.

At WWE, I developed churn models reaching approximately 0.77 AUROC through feature engineering, input transformations, and experiments with deeper neural networks and Gaussian-noise regularization between hidden layers. The work involved testing changes to the inputs and the model rather than treating a deeper network as the entire solution.

More recently, at Podimetrics, I designed Bayesian and weighted-window streaming estimators, tested candidates under drift, outliers, irregular sampling, and cold starts, and translated the selected Python method to C with numerical-equivalence checks for firmware integration. Adaptive estimation reduced the time to reliable patient-specific estimates from roughly 20–30 days to 3–5 days.

I would welcome the opportunity to develop and evaluate modeling ideas with your quantitative research team.

Sincerely,
Kevin Urban, PhD

## Editorial notes

- **Central argument:** Modeling hypothesis, experiment authorship and measured contribution.
- **Why it works:** Clear attribution distinguishes conceived experiments, delegated implementation and personal architecture work. The WWE example distinguishes feature and model experimentation without assigning an uncertain fraction of the improvement to noise injection.
- **Limits:** This short example does not establish financial-market or large-scale distributed-training experience. More research detail may be needed for a different reader.
- **Canonical evidence:** CVB-20, CVB-10, CVB-15, WWE-04, POD-01, POD-02, POD-03.
