Reviewed: 2026-10-23. Role context: Lead Scientific Software Engineer, MoBI, Child Mind Institute.

This is a reviewed writing example, not a replacement for a historical application or a mandatory template. Facts remain governed by the [resume bullet bank](../../RESUME_BULLET_BANK.md).

## Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Lead Scientific Software Engineer, MoBI position at Child Mind Institute. My work combines physiological-sensing research with the software needed to collect, process, and evaluate its measurements. MoBI's responsibility for collection, quality control, and analysis across modalities closely matches work I have led at Cohen Veterans Bioscience and Podimetrics.

At Cohen Veterans Bioscience, I built and led the Sensor Analytics Team and originated the Parkinson's research plan and roadmap that secured a Michael J. Fox Foundation grant and its renewal. I remained hands-on: after repeatedly implementing related biosignal models, I developed SensorNet, an internal pip-installable package with configurable Conv1D-LSTM models, training and inference methods, and time-series utilities. Projects installed documented revisions as the package evolved. I also reviewed researchers' software and modeling pipelines for implementation choices and leakage risks that affected the scientific conclusion.

At Podimetrics, I owned and substantially refactored the production thermal-processing Python pipeline, integrated consultant-supplied quality modules, and adapted algorithms to new hardware. Working with software and hardware engineers, I tested changes in isolated production AWS clones using the team's PR-triggered Terraform and GitHub Actions workflow.

In Rett syndrome monitoring, I co-developed wearable protocols with a hospital partner and audited smartwatch, video, and annotation data for synchronization, timestamp, and coverage problems. In sleep work, I developed a DTW-based agreement framework to account for timing differences between EEG- and motion-based measurements. These are distinctions I would bring to implementation reviews: a software defect, a measurement difference, and a study-design limitation need different responses.

I would welcome the opportunity to help MoBI's researchers and clinicians build dependable tools while mentoring contributors and setting practical standards for scientific software.

Sincerely,
Kevin Urban, PhD

## Editorial notes

- **Central argument:** Scientific-software leadership through agenda, reusable implementation and quality judgment.
- **Why it works:** The funded roadmap, internal package, scientific review and integrated testing demonstrate leadership through work. The software-defect/measurement-difference/study-limitation distinction adds a useful interpretation.
- **Limits:** Team leadership is not evidence of enterprise-wide authority. Use of team-provisioned Terraform environments is not authorship of their infrastructure.
